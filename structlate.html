Detailed Description

Late-phase hazard model parameters.

Stores the four shape parameters for the G3 late-phase shaping function. Initialized by SETG3 / hzd_late_t2p().

The late-phase cumulative hazard is: \( CF_3(t) = \mu_L \cdot G_3(t;\,\tau,\,\gamma,\,\alpha,\,\eta) \)
