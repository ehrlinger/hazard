Detailed Description

Early-phase hazard model parameters.

Stores the four shape parameters plus derived quantities for the G1 early-phase shaping function. Initialized by SETG1 / hzd_early_t2p().

The early-phase cumulative hazard is: \( CF_1(t) = \mu_E \cdot G_1(t;\,\delta,\,t_{1/2},\,\nu,\,M) \)
