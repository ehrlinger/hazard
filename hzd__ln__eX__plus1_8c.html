double hzd_ln_eX_plus1 ( double  X)

Compute \(\ln(e^x + 1)\) with series expansion for small \(x\).

For \(x \leq -174\), \(e^x \approx 0\) and the result is 0. For \(-174 < x < 5\), uses the alternating series \(\ln(1+z) = z - z^2/2 + z^3/3 - \cdots\) with \(z = e^x\). For \(x \geq 5\), \(e^x \gg 1\) and the result is returned as \(x\).
